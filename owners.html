New Items waiting

Items added at a store that are not placed yet. Place each one as suggested, place it on a different list, or reject it. This queue and the workbook queue are the only places items are reviewed — never the store app. Reject is only for items that were never placed.

Live catalogue — discontinue

Discontinue is for items that ARE placed and live. A discontinued item is Phasing Out: it is still counted while stock runs down, it comes off shopping and ordering right away, and it stays known-but-closed so it is never re-added as a new item. Bring back reverses it.
