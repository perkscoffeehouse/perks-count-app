A customer's card says “invalid” on Toast? No problem. Lots of our older cards were never loaded into Toast. This looks the card up on the old list and gets its real balance.

1. Scan each card's barcode — flip the card over, the number is a barcode on the back. A photo is saved as we go.

2. See the balance. Cards can be combined into one total.

3. Use the balance: make one new card on Toast for the total and scan it here — or take the amount off their order as a discount instead. Small balances don't need a new card.
